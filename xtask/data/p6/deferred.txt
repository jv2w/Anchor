# P6 deferral table: packets BDS sends in the spawn window that Anchor never sends, each with
# its reason. `cargo xtask p6 compare` removes them from both captures. Anchor sends every packet
# of the window the official client may need; a packet is listed only when it is tied to
# something Anchor does not have or does nothing the client needs.
# <packet> <status> <reason>; status `-`: Anchor never sends the packet.

# The recipe book: BDS 1.26.52 unlocks entries right after the window (UnlockedRecipes type 1,
# 20 recipes, `xtask/data/p6/facts.md`) and after each craft. With `recipe-book` on, Anchor marks
# every recipe of its CraftingData always unlocked, so the client shows them all without unlocks,
# as with PMMP.
UnlockedRecipes - recipe book unlocks; every recipe Anchor sends is always unlocked, as PMMP

# Vanilla mobs: BDS spawns and despawns animals in the flat world during the spawn window;
# Anchor simulates no mobs, so it never sends these there. The
# SetActorData and UpdateAttributes BDS sends for those mobs are left out by `compare.rs`
# (`ENTITY_DATA`: only the player's own are compared).
AddActor - the flat world's natural mobs; Anchor spawns no mobs
RemoveActor - the flat world's natural mobs despawning; Anchor spawns no mobs
MoveActorDelta - natural mob movement; Anchor spawns no mobs
MoveActorAbsolute - natural mob movement; Anchor spawns no mobs
SetActorMotion - natural mob movement; Anchor spawns no mobs
ActorEvent - natural mob events; Anchor spawns no mobs
MobEquipment - mob equipment, sent only for mobs and villagers in view, never in the spawn window
MobArmourEquipment - mob armour, sent only for mobs and villagers in view, never in the spawn window
AddItemActor - items the flat world's mobs drop (chicken eggs); Anchor spawns no mobs, and no item lies near a new world's spawn

# Sounds: in the window BDS plays the entity "spawn" sound for the joining player (EntityType
# minecraft:player at 0,0,0 with its first chunks) and for each mob it adds. A sound the client
# needs neither to join nor to play; Anchor plays no entity spawn sounds (PMMP and dragonfly neither).
LevelSoundEvent - entity spawn sounds (the joining player at the origin, mobs); no join or game effect
